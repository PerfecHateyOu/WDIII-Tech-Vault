# Experiment 11: Lenovo Googlebook 15 & Google Pixel Integration

A plain review of how well the Lenovo Googlebook 15 integrates with a Google Pixel. Status: done.

## Session & Setup Day

- Googlebook OS version: Google Book OS.
- Android version: Android 17 (noted as the operating system required for cross-device functionality).
- Googlebook model / config: Lenovo Googlebook 15, featuring a magnesium alloy build, soft-touch finish, and 2.8K OLED display.
- Phone: Pixel 10 Pro with Android 17.

## Reliability

- Features that failed: Wi-Fi connectivity, clipboard management, cross-device app streaming, and task continuation.
- Notes: Phone integration setup proved "very janky" and inconsistent. The OS lacks desktop-class application support, forcing users to rely on mobile apps stretched for large screens rather than full-featured professional tools.

## Incidents

- Feature: Wi-Fi. Description: Frequent connectivity issues.
- Feature: Interface. Description: Crashing interfaces.
- Feature: Clipboard. Description: Poor clipboard management.
- Feature: Phone Integration. Description: App streaming and task continuation remain highly inconsistent and restricted to specific devices.

## Notes

- Anything else from this session: AI integrations like the "wiggle to activate" Gemini shortcut and on-device file descriptions are neat but not competitive "killer apps." At a price point of $900 to $1,300, the platform feels like a first-generation product that is currently only suitable for dedicated early adopters, struggling to compete with established options like macOS.
