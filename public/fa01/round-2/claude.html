How we test

Each experiment follows the same four stages, so results from different months can sit side by side.

1

Write the question

One measurable question per experiment, fixed before any device or service is touched.

2

Buy it ourselves

Retail units and paid services, bought anonymously. No review samples, no press appointments.

3

Run it three times

Scripted or timed runs, repeated at least three times. We publish the median.

4

Publish the doubts

Every result ships with the conditions that could change it and the sample size behind it.

No scores out of ten

We report the unit that was measured: hours, minutes, pounds, percent. You decide what matters.

Winners are per test

A phone can win battery life and lose charging speed. We never roll results into one ranking.

Old results stay up

When software updates change a result, we rerun the test and keep both dates on the page.

Corrections are dated

If we got something wrong, the fix and the date appear on the experiment itself.
