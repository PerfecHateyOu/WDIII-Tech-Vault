Illustrative experiment · Smartphones

Does a bigger battery survive a real workday?

Three anonymized phones were configured with matching brightness targets, the same messaging account, and a fixed sequence of calls, maps, camera use, and video playback.

Finding: the largest battery did not produce the longest usable session in this sample. Network conditions and background activity created larger swings than the battery-capacity numbers suggested.

The result is a design example for this website, not a current product benchmark.

Method before opinion

The site treats reproducibility as a feature: every conclusion should point back to a protocol someone else could inspect.

01 · ControlKeep settings, network conditions, task order, and charging state as consistent as practical.
02 · RepeatRun important measurements several times and show variation instead of hiding inconvenient runs.
03 · DocumentSeparate measured data from observations and interpretation; label estimates and illustrative content.

A documentation site, not a shopping list.

Field Notes is designed for readers who want to understand how consumer technology behaves in context. Each report can contain a protocol, raw notes, photos, repair invoices, test conditions, and a short interpretation.

Editorial rule: no single number is treated as the whole story. Product names, prices, repair policies, and software versions should be verified for the date of each published experiment.
