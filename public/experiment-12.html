Experiment 12

In Progress

iOS 27 Performance & AI Chatbot Integration on iPhone 16e

Device iPhone 16e
Model Code iPhone17,5
OS Primary iOS 27.0 (final)
Tracking Later iOS 27 updates as released

Research Objective

Test Apple's iOS 27 claims on iPhone 16e — including the claimed "up to 60%" improvement in app responsiveness — through app launch timing, standby battery drain and crash counts. Once Gemini, Claude and ChatGPT gain third-party Siri support, compare all three through Siri with identical prompts.

Scope & Methodology

Testing Parameters

Device: iPhone 16e (Model iPhone17,5)

OS Versions: iOS 27.0; later iOS 27 updates tracked as they are released

Environment: Temperature-controlled room (about 21°C)

Results describe this one iPhone 16e and are not generalised to other iPhone models. The same phone is used for Experiment 10, which keeps a separate log.

Test Procedures

A. iOS 27 Claims

Apple's claims being tested include an "up to 60%" improvement in app responsiveness.

1. Responsiveness

  • Cold and warm app launch times, timed with a stopwatch

2. Battery

  • Overnight standby drain: start %, end % and hours — same method as Experiment 10

3. Stability

  • Count of new crash logs in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Analytics Data — same method as Experiment 10

B. AI Chatbots via Siri — Not Started

This part starts once Gemini, Claude and ChatGPT get third-party Siri support, which is expected within the next couple of months.

  • Identical prompts sent to all three through Siri
  • Compared on latency, handoff reliability and multi-turn context

C. Headline Features — TBD

Which iOS 27 headline features to verify is still to be decided.

Data Collection & Current Status

⚡ Testing underway — no results yet

iOS 27.0 testing on iPhone 16e has started. Results will be added here when there is data to report. The AI chatbot tests have not started.

Limitations

Documented Limitations

  • Single device (n=1): findings describe this iPhone 16e only.
  • Stopwatch timing: can only catch large differences in launch times.
  • AI chatbot timing: depends on when third-party Siri support ships.
  • Shared device: the same phone is used for Experiment 10, which keeps a separate log.
